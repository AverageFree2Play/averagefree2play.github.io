My Work

Projects
I have worked on various projects, some are definitely helpful in some way, others are just my small apps that you can goof around with. Some of my notable projects include:
  • SpookSounds - A .net console application that emulates the old "spooky" sounds played in the background of old horror games.

Questions & Answers
Inevitably, people will ask questions, so I want to get a few out of the way that I think would be the most common, so you already have an answer, in no particular order.
Do you accept friend requests on various platforms?
Not really. If you see me randomly accepting for no particular reason, it's likely either a mistake, curiosity, or a resolving a certain issue.
I prefer to know the person before adding them as a friend. This usually involves interacting with them in some capacity first. I'm not gonna accept your friend request if you just send it and didn't say anything.

Can I get you to do something for me in terms of work?
I don't really do work for people. I have my own projects that I work on, and I don't really have the time to do work for other people. If you want to hire me for something, you can contact me and we can discuss it.

Can you teach me how to code?
As long as it's within my scope, I can help you learn to code! I'm always happy to share my knowledge and experience with those who are interested in learning. However, keep in mind that I don't offer formal tutoring or teaching services.
Though anything that I'm not aware of you may need to ask someone else or look into official documentation.

Can I use your work for my own project?
Yes, you can use my work for your own project, as long as you give me credit for it. You can also modify my work to fit your needs, but please don't claim it as your own.
All my open-source projects are licensed under the MIT License and CC BY-SA 4.0.

Do you take commissions or payments for your work?
I don't typically take commissions or payments for my work. I create projects for fun and to share with the community. However, If something is part of a larger project or collaboration, I'd prefer if you'd pay for it.

Do you make videos?
If you already saw my youtube channel then, I don't usually make videos. All my videos are either gameplay footage, showcases and other random content.

That's all of the questions I can think of for now. If you have a particular question that doesn't fell in any of the categories above, you can contact me.
